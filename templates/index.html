How MindPulse Works

01

Text Cleaning

Removes URLs, mentions, and special characters. Handles both English and Roman Urdu normalization.

02

RoBERTa Encoding

Twitter-fine-tuned RoBERTa tokenizes and encodes the cleaned text into dense 768-dim contextualized embeddings.

03

NRC Lexicon Fusion

10-dimensional affect scores from the NRC Word Emotion Lexicon are extracted with negation-aware context windows.

04

Sarcasm & Calibration

An AI irony classifier and regex pattern matching detect sarcasm and calibrate the final prediction accordingly.

05

Risk Assessment

Multi-signal composite scores per psychological dimension produce a final risk level from Low → High.
